SAT Prep

This covers the Digital SAT — the computer-based version of the SAT that's given at test centers today. It's scored 400–1600 and split into two sections.

SectionModulesQuestionsTime
Reading & Writing2 (32 min each)54 total64 minutes
Math2 (35 min each)44 total70 minutes

Total test time is about 2 hours 14 minutes, with a 10-minute break between sections. Each section is adaptive — how you do on the first module decides whether your second module is easier or harder, which affects your score.

How to use this site: Go through each domain below, read the explanation, work the example, then use the official practice links to drill that skill with real questions.
